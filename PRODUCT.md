# PRODUCT.md

## Register
product

## Platform
web (static, offline-capable, GitHub Pages deployable)

## Audience
Readers and word-puzzle enthusiasts who want a calm, literate archive for daily word games, private two-reader pass-and-play matches on one device, and sourced etymology stories without accounts, trackers, or noisy gamification.

## Product Purpose
Provide nine distinct word-puzzle modes backed by a 20,220-word archive alongside an independent Education reading room of 36 sourced word histories, a personal record ranked by the reader's own calendar days, and an unscored Zen setting.

## Principles
- **Calm by default.** No ticking clocks unless playing a scored Battle match; Zen removes all clocks, streaks, and scores.
- **Separation of play and reading.** Education is an independent reading room with zero game chrome, timers, or scores.
- **Private on-device record.** The personal leaderboard ranks the reader's own calendar days on their device, never other people.
- **Tactile archival craft.** Every surface, tile, transition, and typographic detail behaves like a well-printed reference folio.
