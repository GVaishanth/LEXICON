How to Play

Archive edition

Word games: Guess in six attempts. Viridian is correctly placed; ochre is elsewhere in the answer.

Daily & Time Machine: A fresh daily puzzle at local midnight; revisit earlier dates without changing your scored daily record.

Battle: Two readers, the same word, private boards. Each submits one guess, reviews their own clues, and conceals the screen before handing it over. The second reader gets the same number of attempts if the first solves.

Hard Mode: Reuse revealed letters and positions.

Zen: Calm, unscored play without clocks or ranking.

Education: A separate, offline collection of 36 sourced word histories. Read without playing.
